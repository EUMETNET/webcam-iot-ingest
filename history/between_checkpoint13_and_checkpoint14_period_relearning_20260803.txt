Deterministic period relearning between checkpoints 13 and 14
=============================================================

Purpose
-------

Prevent an anomalous historical minimum from trapping
`estimated_source_stream_period` indefinitely, without adding persistent
counters or another database field.

Implementation
--------------

The three ingestion networks share one SHA-256/modulo decision based on the
source-stream ID and canonical provider timestamp. Normally, their established
period update remains unchanged. On one deterministic event in N, the latest
positive provider-timestamp gap directly replaces the estimate. N is configured
independently per network and defaults to 250 for Windy, Fintraffic, and
Skaping.

Direct replacement requires a pre-existing estimate. Estimates reset to NULL
therefore pass through normal initial learning first. Epoch 1, initial stagger
release, and epochs lasting at least the minimum ingestion interval continue
to suppress estimate application. Updates remain batched at epoch end.

Observability
-------------

`webcam_ingestion_period_estimate_update_total` records applied updates with
the bounded method label `initial`, `recurrent`, or `direct_replacement`.
Each ingestion Grafana dashboard shows 30-minute increases by method.

Validation experiment
---------------------

The dedicated command uses N=10 for a 30-minute full three-network run:

    just period-replacement-test 30m 10

Before worker startup it explicitly resets only the period column. The reset
preserves provider, download, and processed timestamps. The initial live reset
cleared 24,752 Windy, 2,180 Fintraffic, and 40 Skaping estimates. After all
workers had been healthy for 39 seconds, every estimate remained NULL and no
period-update metric existed, confirming startup/epoch 1 did not interfere
with initial learning.

During the subsequent run, Prometheus observed initial learning before any
direct replacement. The later applied updates included:

    network  recurrent  direct replacement  direct share of later updates
    win          23,430               2,602                         10.00%
    fin           2,739                 317                         10.37%
    ska              32                   2                          5.88%

Windy and Fintraffic therefore match the intended one-in-ten validation
frequency. Skaping produced only 15 later updates in this observation, which
is sufficient to prove the path is active but too small for a meaningful
frequency estimate. At the final snapshot, initial learning had been applied
to 23,239 Windy, 2,160 Fintraffic, and 33 Skaping streams. The runner then
stopped all three validation workers and completed successfully. These
observations validate that deterministic replacement follows rather than interferes with
initial learning. The production default remains one in 250 per network.
