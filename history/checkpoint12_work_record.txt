Checkpoint 12 work record
=========================

Scope
-----

Checkpoint 12 implements bounded operational validation rather than the final
production schedule. Recovery drills and global systemd deployment belong to
checkpoint 13.

Implemented material
--------------------

- `storage.s3_spool_cleanup` selects only canonical derived-image keys older
  than an exact cutoff. It supports dry-run and bounded deletion, excludes
  unknown and backup keys, paginates listings, batches deletes, and emits
  persistent cleanup metrics.
- `database.database_backup` produces a full PostgreSQL custom-format dump,
  uploads it below `backups/postgresql/`, verifies size and SHA-256 metadata,
  and emits persistent backup metrics. It never restores a dump.
- Pushgateway metrics and the Operational batch jobs dashboard cover cleanup
  and backup outcome, duration, stages, objects, and byte volume.
- postgres_exporter, node_exporter, and cAdvisor are pinned in Compose and
  scraped by Prometheus. Infrastructure and validation-worker alert rules and
  the Infrastructure health dashboard are provisioned.
- Validation-only systemd units run three five-job-bounded continuous workers
  and a non-overlapping ten-minute maintenance cycle. Discovery is dry-run in
  Windy, Fintraffic, Skaping order, with Windy restricted to Denmark. It is
  followed by real one-hour spool cleanup and a real database backup.

Safety boundaries
-----------------

- Cleanup derives age only from a fully validated immutable image key.
- Database backups cannot match the cleanup key grammar.
- The first live cleanup must begin with a dry-run; the exact bounded deletion
  scope must be shown to the owner before deletion.
- Validation units are not production scheduling policy.
- Database restoration and destructive volume operations are absent.
