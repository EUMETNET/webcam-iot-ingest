Quiet three-network baseline between checkpoints 12 and 13 (2026-08-01)
===========================================================================

Purpose
-------

This 90-minute run checked that the retained Windy polling configuration could
operate beside reduced-resource Fintraffic and Skaping workers while two
sequential discovery-and-cleanup cycles ran on the same eight-core VM.

Configuration
-------------

- Windy: 6 CPUs, 84 worker threads, batched freshness, bounded-minimum source
  period estimator, 9-minute polling floor, lambda 0.7, and deterministic
  10-minute initial staggering.
- Fintraffic: 0.5 CPU, 4 worker threads, and an 8-minute polling floor.
- Skaping: 0.5 CPU, 2 worker threads, and a 4-minute polling floor.
- Maintenance: one container limited to 0.5 CPU. At T+20 and T+60 minutes it
  ran Windy, Fintraffic, and Skaping discovery sequentially, followed by
  cleanup of T0V0 objects older than 24 hours.

Outcome
-------

All four containers exited with status 0. Both maintenance cycles completed
without failed deletions or discovery failures.

| Result | Windy | Fintraffic | Skaping |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Completed epochs | 165 | 296 | 360 |
| Published images | 194,521 | 15,844 | 276 |
| Publications per 5 minutes | 10,807 | 880 | 15 |
| P50 epoch duration | 7.81 s | 1.74 s | 2.23 s |
| P90 epoch duration | 108.98 s | 30.44 s | 3.55 s |
| P95 epoch duration | 146.25 s | 35.91 s | 4.02 s |
| Maximum epoch duration | 238.61 s | 66.71 s | 5.16 s |
| Download errors | 8 | 4,703 | 0 |
| Failed batched freshness requests | 0 | not applicable | not applicable |
| Throttled batched freshness requests | 0 | not applicable | not applicable |

The first maintenance cycle lasted about 26 minutes. Its cleanup removed an
existing backlog of 441,972 objects (8.90 GB) without failures. The second
cycle lasted about 10 minutes 49 seconds and removed 3,512 objects (63.4 MB),
also without failures. The second Windy discovery inserted no streams and
made no activation changes after the first pass.

Windy throughput comparison
----------------------------

| Experiment | Completed MQTT publications per 5 minutes |
|---|---:|
| 2026-07-23 steady window | 10,269 |
| Later documented two-hour Windy run | 10,013 |
| Adaptive polling without a floor | 10,751 |
| No adaptive polling | 10,721 |
| 9-minute polling floor | 10,833 |
| Quiet three-network baseline | 10,807 |

The quiet baseline was only 0.24 percent below the preceding 9-minute-floor
experiment. Concurrent Fintraffic and Skaping ingestion plus discovery and
cleanup therefore caused no material loss of Windy publication throughput.

Latency interpretation
----------------------

Prometheus did not retain the historical series from these one-off worker
containers after they exited. A database snapshot over 23,767 Windy streams
whose final recorded download occurred during the run gave provider-to-download
P50 157.74 seconds, P90 207.83 seconds, P95 230.47 seconds, and mean 298.19
seconds.

Those values are one terminal observation per stream. They are not directly
comparable with the earlier Prometheus values, which included every publication
in a selected time window. In particular, the database P95 of 3.84 minutes
must not be interpreted as a demonstrated improvement over the earlier final
10-minute Prometheus P95 of 4.78 minutes. Throughput stability is demonstrated;
latency improvement is not.

Conclusion
----------

The resource allocation and sequential maintenance arrangement are suitable
as the quiet baseline for checkpoint-13 recovery work. Container restart and
recovery tests should retain this workload shape. The significant Fintraffic
download-error rate remains a separate provider/download investigation rather
than an orchestration failure.
